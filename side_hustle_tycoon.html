Goal: Build up your side hustles, unlock upgrades, and eventually prestige to become a true money tycoon.

  • Tap to Hustle: Click the big button to earn instant cash. Tap upgrades increase this amount.
  • Side Gigs: Buy gigs like freelancing, online shops, or rental income. They pay you automatically every second.
  • Upgrades: Each upgrade has a cost and a level. Higher levels give more power but cost more.
  • Boosts: Use the 30‑second income boost to temporarily multiply your earnings.
  • Prestige: Once you’ve earned enough total money, you can reset for permanent multipliers that make future runs much faster.
  • Streaks: Keep tapping regularly to maintain a streak bonus that slightly boosts your tap value.
  • Save & Load: Use the buttons to store your progress in your browser and come back later.

Experiment with different strategies: rush tap upgrades, stack passive gigs, or prestige early and often. The loop is designed to feel snappy and addictive.
